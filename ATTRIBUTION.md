app's dictionary and learning content comes from openly licensed sources; the notices below apply to the content
visible in the screenshots and to the assets in this repository.

## Audio

No audio files are redistributed in this repository. The app streams recordings from Wikimedia Commons; each file is
licensed per file (mostly CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY 3.0 or CC0) with author and licence shown in the app's source list.

## AI-generated content

Some example sentences and editorial edits were produced with an AI model and are labelled in the app. The learning
material (puzzles, reading texts, exam topics, role-plays, writing tasks) is original content generated for this
project with AI assistance and reviewed for structure and language before integration. It contains no third-party
quotations.
