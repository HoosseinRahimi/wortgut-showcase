Look up, understand, practice.

The dictionary and the learning path share one dataset: every example, grammar table and exercise is built from the same 5,000 entries, so what you look up is what you practice.

Search that understands German

Umlauts, inflected forms, compounds and separable verbs. Results keep their context instead of dropping you into a bare word list.

More than a definition

Meanings, word classes, gender and plural, declension and conjugation, IPA, hyphenation, synonyms, frequency rank and example sentences.

Spaced repetition

Save words to your own list and review them as flashcards with an SM-2 schedule, a daily goal and progress statistics.

A guided course

Six units from A1 to B2 combine vocabulary, one grammar focus and a short application. A placement test builds a personal daily plan.

Exercises with explanations

Articles, plurals, verb forms and gap texts, plus 51 word-order puzzles, 18 annotated case examples and exam vocabulary.

Pronunciation, offline

6,320 verified Wikimedia recordings with IPA, per-file attribution and a fallback to the device voice. The dictionary works without a connection.

Under the hood

A small product with a deliberately boring stack — built so the data can be audited, not just shipped.

Product

  • React 19 + TypeScript, esbuild, hand-written service worker and design system
  • Kotlin + Jetpack Compose Android app with DataStore and offline assets
  • Installable PWA, dark mode, reduced-motion setting, keyboard and screen-reader support
  • Optional web accounts (Supabase) and Stripe billing for the Plus tier
  • Optional AI coach against a user-supplied endpoint and key

Data & quality

  • Python pipeline builds, audits and exports the dictionary and attribution sidecars
  • Provenance matching against pinned Wiktionary revisions: 12,920 of 12,961 examples matched
  • Content hash-bound review workspace and release-evidence gate (29,644 review units)
  • Unit tests across web, pipeline and Android; prebuilt deploy with post-deploy verification
  • Vercel hosting; static, offline-first delivery
